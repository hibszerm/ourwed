Auth email previews

Branded OurWed authentication emails. Sample confirmation URLs are substituted for browser review only — production templates keep {{ .ConfirmationURL }}.

  • Zmień hasło do konta OurWed (recovery)
  • Potwierdź adres e-mail (confirmation)
  • Zaloguj się do OurWed (magic_link)
  • Potwierdź zmianę adresu e-mail (email_change)
  • Zaproszenie do OurWed (invite)

Tip: resize the browser to ~390px width to check mobile layout. Toggle OS dark mode to check Apple Mail dark rendering.
